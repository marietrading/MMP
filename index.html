Welcome to Our Community

Welcome to the digital home of Medalla Milagrosa Parish. We are a community of faith, hope, and love, dedicated to serving God and our neighbors through the grace of the Miraculous Medal.

Our Six Ministries

Worship & Liturgy

Enhancing our spiritual experience through sacred music, altar service, and liturgical preparation.

Education & Catechesis

Guiding our children, youth, and adults in the deepening of their Catholic faith.

Social Action

Serving the community through outreach, charity, and social justice initiatives.

Youth & Young Adult

Empowering the next generation to lead and grow within the church community.

Family Life

Strengthening marriages and family-centered activities for the domestic church.

Pastoral Care

Providing comfort and the sacraments to the sick and the homebound.
